# LCEReborn Live 1.3.0 — Village & Pillage

This release brings the latest Village & Pillage work from the PS4 Legacy Edition / Minecraft 1.14 update to LCEReborn, with updated villagers, trading, workstations, raids and world generation.

## Villagers and trading

- Updated villager appearances, professions and daily routines: villagers visit their job sites during the day and return home at night.
- Job-site blocks let unemployed villagers take up professions. Trading includes profession-specific offers, five trading levels, experience and restocking at work sites.
- Updated the trading interface to follow the PS4 Legacy Edition layout.
- Added the Wandering Trader and trader llamas.

## Workstations and blocks

- Cartography Table for map crafting, Grindstone for repairs and disenchanting, and Stonecutter for stone recipes.
- Barrel storage, faster food cooking in the Smoker, faster ore smelting in the Blast Furnace, and crop composting into Bone Meal.
- Loom for banner patterns, Lectern, Bell, Lantern, Campfire, Scaffolding, Bamboo and Sweet Berry Bushes.
- Smithing and Fletching Tables serve as villager job sites, matching this version of the game.
- Adapted PS4 menus, block models, textures and sounds for the new content.

## Mobs, raids and exploration

- Pillagers, Ravagers, patrols and village raids, including raid captains, Bad Omen, the raid progress display and Hero of the Village rewards.
- Crossbow gameplay and enchantment support.
- Cats and pandas, with their models and behaviours.
- Village & Pillage village layouts for plains, desert, savanna, taiga and snowy biomes, plus pillager outposts.
- Fixed village placement on terrain, villager spawning, structure rotation and structure save/load issues. Generation fixes apply to newly generated areas.

## Installation

All required game assets are included in the Windows installer and the Windows/Linux packages. No separate game-data download or import is needed. The separate 1.14 Texture Pack has been removed from the distribution; this does not remove the new Village & Pillage game content.

## Downloads

Choose one client download for your platform. Each file includes its own game assets; you do not need to download the other packages.

- **Windows installer:** `LCEReborn-Live-Installer-1.3.0.exe`.
- **Windows portable:** `LCEReborn-Live-Portable-1.3.0.zip`.
- **Linux AppImage:** `LCEReborn-Live-Linux-1.3.0.AppImage`.
- **Linux portable:** `LCEReborn-Live-Linux-Portable-1.3.0.tar.gz`.
- **Dedicated servers:** `LCEReborn-Live-Server-Windows-1.3.0.zip` and `LCEReborn-Live-Server-Linux-1.3.0.tar.gz`. These are only needed for hosting a dedicated server.

Keep the `assets` directory beside the launcher when using a portable package. The installer places it automatically.
